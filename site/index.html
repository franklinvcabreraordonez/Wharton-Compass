    Courses

    Tell us how you like to work and we rank courses to match. Ratings are Penn Course Review averages on a 0 to 4 scale. They are rough guides, not guarantees. When you have picked courses, build your schedule in Penn Course Plan.

    How to read this: higher difficulty and workload mean harder and heavier. The data has no review counts, so treat small differences as noise. Graduate courses (5000 and up) are hidden unless you pick them under Level, and searching a course code always finds it. Courses come from the last several terms, so a course that has not run recently may be missing. Interest labels on courses are rule-based guesses from the department and keywords. This tab does not check degree requirements, so confirm them in Path@Penn degree audit or with your advisor.
